Variance Reduced Pricing for Path-Dependent Options

Used Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM) to simulate risk-neutral future price paths, monitored the paths for barrier breaches and autocall events, applied the corresponding payoff conditions, and discounted the resulting cash flows using the appropriate risk-free rate to estimate the ABRC price.
