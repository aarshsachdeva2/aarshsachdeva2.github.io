Fixed Income

Treasury Yield Curve Forecasting

Developed a Dynamic Nelson–Siegel framework capturing level, slope and curvature, and benchmarked it against direct VECM modeling on raw Treasury yields.

6.5 bps cross-sectional RMSE with DNS and 4.0 bps with direct VECM in the cited resume version; another version reports a 38% one-step RMSE reduction from 6.5 to 4.0 bps.

Monte Carlo · Option Pricing

Variance-Reduced Pricing for Path-Dependent Options

Engineered a variance-reduced Monte Carlo engine using Control Variates and Antithetic Variates, then implemented Sobol-based Quasi-Monte Carlo.

Reported 10–21% standard-error reduction versus Vanilla MC, and 100× SE reduction / 55× cost reduction with Sobol QMC at equivalent simulation budget.
